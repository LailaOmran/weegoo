Privacy Policy

After Gomaa Praying · Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game After Gomaa Praying by WeeGoo ("we", "us") collects and how it is used. In short: the game does not collect any personal data.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The game does not show ads, does not include analytics or tracking tools, and does not send any data to our servers. It plays fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (which maps you have finished and the results of your last run on each map), whether you have seen the tutorial, and your settings (such as music and sound volume) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the game removes them.

Tracking

The game does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us through App Store Connect. These do not identify you, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you have any concern, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data (for example ads, online play or leaderboards), we will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
