Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Claim It Arena and other games by WeeGoo ("we", "us") collect and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run our own servers that store personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, level, gems and settings (such as sound on or off) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the game removes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads (interstitial ads between rounds and optional rewarded ads) through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation service), which connects the game to advertising networks. To show and measure ads, Unity and these ad networks may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or the Android advertising ID);
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked and completed).

This data is used to deliver and measure ads, to show ads that may be more relevant to you, and to prevent fraud. We do not control how these companies process data. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for details.

Tracking choice (iOS)

On iOS the game asks for your permission before allowing tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see ads that are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity through its privacy policy page; you can also contact us and we will help.
  • Where required, the game shows a consent screen first, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings, if available.

Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through our games, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the new version.
