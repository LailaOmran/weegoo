Privacy Policy

To My Husband · Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game To My Husband by WeeGoo ("we", "us") collects and how it is used. In short: we do not collect personal data ourselves. The only data that leaves your device is handled by our advertising partner when you choose to watch a rewarded ad.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run our own servers that store personal data. The game does not include our own analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (the level you have reached), your stars, the outfits you have bought or chosen in the shop, and your settings (sound and vibration on or off) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the game removes them.

Advertising

The game shows only optional rewarded ads: after finishing a level you can choose to watch a video to earn extra stars. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. Rewarded ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation service), which connects the game to advertising networks. To show and measure ads, Unity and these ad networks may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), only if you allow tracking;
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked and completed).

This data is used to deliver and measure ads, to show ads that may be more relevant to you, and to prevent fraud. We do not control how these companies process data. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for details.

Tracking choice (iOS)

Your advertising identifier is only shared if you allow the game to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you have not allowed tracking, or choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not shared and the ads you see are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us through App Store Connect. These do not identify you, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Your choices and rights

  • Watching ads is always optional: you can play the whole game without watching any.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity through its privacy policy page; you can also contact us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if a future version adds new features that handle data. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the new version.
